Entry for the [One-Line Laugh Sprint](https://make.fun/c/u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-laugh-sprint-cda708b2) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one hour, one original funny line of up to 200 characters on everyday life. Free to enter, $5 prize pool, an AI judge ranks the lines and the top places get paid.

## The entry

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes one original funny line about everyday life to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My smartwatch congratulated me on 10,000 steps, most of them pacing the kitchen while I decided what to eat.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
